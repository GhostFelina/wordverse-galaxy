# Roadmap

## Current desktop development

Complete the Orion/stars visual review, camera reliability, useful atlas/recall flow, local card export and repository presentation. Development previews remain distinct from the published app.

## Next product work

- Persistent recall scheduling with transparent progress semantics.
- Broader real-browser/GPU coverage, longer sessions and large vocabulary collections.
- Account synchronization acceptance across two devices, with recoverable conflicts.
- A dedicated mobile design and touch performance review.

## Explorations

Additional nebulae and catalog objects may return once the vocabulary experience is stable and legible. They remain disabled today. Optional artistic sky experiences are distinct from measured catalog data and are not a promise of a physics engine.

Engineering work is tracked in [tasks](handoff/TASKS.md). No dates or platform-support guarantees are implied.
