# Nebula exploration

Version1.27 retains Orion as the default, three original independently baked volumes and ten observation-guided popular nebula experiences. All fourteen use the same bounded raymarch renderer, display pipeline, density/light controls, reversible camera travel and personal star presentation. Selecting a distant preview never starts flight. Explicit entry hides the home, exposes flight depth and retains the return action.

The original128³ RGBA fields encode density, approximate illumination, dust and color. They reuse the64³ fine-detail field. Manifest dimensions, decompressed lengths and SHA256 identify their payloads. Ion Veil is generated by `scripts/build-ion-veil.mjs`; the other two by `scripts/build-nebula-experiences.mjs`. New density and full guide downloads occur on explicit selection. An LRU retains at most three alternate volume entries alongside Orion; eviction disposes both gas/detail and guide textures. Successful selections are reused while cached. They do not block Orion's initial load. Optional failures preserve the current scene and allow retry. A revision guard rejects results that complete after return, another selection or scene disposal. Star extinction reads the same currently active field as the gas material. Owned textures are disposed once with the scene.

The three density payloads total approximately17.3MB compressed and are loaded independently. Fine detail, texture dimensions, adaptive buffer resolution and available GPU quality determine visible texture fidelity. These are original artistic reconstructions, not measured astronomical objects or calibrated tomography.

The desktop preview picker opens visibly with two-column image cards, a fourteen-scene total, translated name/catalog search and a ten-popular-nebula filter. The shared searchable atlas contains all fourteen scenes, descriptions and complete linked credits. Both entry points use the same pending/failure/retry selection path. Compact menus isolate scrolling and keep entry/return reachable. Loading, failure and retry stay at the selector.
