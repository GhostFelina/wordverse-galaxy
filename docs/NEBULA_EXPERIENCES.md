# Nebula exploration

Version1.26 retains Orion as the default scene and adds three original, independently baked volumes. All four use the same bounded raymarch renderer, display pipeline, density/light controls, reversible camera travel and personal star presentation. Selecting a distant preview never starts flight. Explicit entry hides the home, exposes flight depth and retains the return action.

| Scene | Structure | Appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orion | Existing observation-guided gas valleys | Copper and blue |
| Ion Veil | Unequal warped ionized sheets, filaments and open gaps | Turquoise, pale light and copper |
| Ember Reach | Three eroded, twisting dusty columns with unequal heads | Crimson, amber and dark dust |
| Tidal Blue | Corrugated ellipsoidal wind shell and empty cavity | Deep blue and pale cyan |

The original128³ RGBA fields encode density, approximate illumination, dust and color. They reuse the64³ fine-detail field. Manifest dimensions, decompressed lengths and SHA256 identify their payloads. Ion Veil is generated by `scripts/build-ion-veil.mjs`; the other two by `scripts/build-nebula-experiences.mjs`. New density downloads occur on explicit selection and successful selections are reused during the session. They do not block Orion's initial load. Optional failures preserve the current scene and allow retry. A revision guard rejects results that complete after return, another selection or scene disposal. Star extinction reads the same currently active field as the gas material. Owned textures are disposed once with the scene.

The three density payloads total approximately17.3MB compressed and are loaded independently. Fine detail, texture dimensions, adaptive buffer resolution and available GPU quality determine visible texture fidelity. These are original artistic reconstructions, not measured astronomical objects or calibrated tomography.

## Interface

The preview picker presents the four scenes with native translated names, visual swatches and short structure descriptions. Loading, failure and retry stay at the selector. The home retains the local display fonts, warm primary actions and restrained borders, with a quieter navigation pill and secondary action.

The interface-language selector shows Türkçe, English and Español, a globe and a selected-state mark. Its menu supports ArrowUp/ArrowDown, Home/End, Escape, focus return and outside dismissal. Locale links are available without waiting for GPU, storage or account initialization. The original select remains a fallback. Existing locale routing handles reload and preserves vocabulary storage. Native language names remain consistent in all locales.
