# Visual specification

## Direction

A calm, cinematic learning atlas: near-black space, an observationally inspired Orion gas field, restrained white/lavender optical points and warm granular stellar surfaces. Serif display typography contrasts with compact, readable controls. Stars and records are the primary hierarchy; decorative elements remain secondary.

## Rendering boundaries

- Current Orion color guidance is an AI-edited, starless native1254×1254 derivative of telescope imagery. No8K, tomography or calibrated astrophysical simulation claim. Depth/volume and stellar convection are artistic procedural models.
- Camera far boundary160/FOV75; random positions are created once and persisted. Never re-layout old records for a screenshot.
- Distant word stars use gentle independent scintillation: bounded light modulation, stationary cores, record-stable phases and projected-size fade. Reduced motion freezes the light; nearby optical bodies and surface texture remain distinct.
- Far optical spikes → mid optical core/halo → near granular photosphere share one body and continuous projected-size LOD. Surface pool≤24; background non-record star layers stay off.
- Selected-target near boundary is radius×1.35 with floating-point safety. Plain wheel never implicitly starts nebula flight.
- A session-only shuffled director starts after a restrained opening interval, separates finite events with quiet gaps and fades them in/out. Manual selection takes priority and quiet mode stops automatic direction. No overlapping flash trains or word-star explosions. The black-hole disk is illustrative, not a gravitational camera/record displacement.
- The black-hole accent has a small apparent size, a distant camera-relative anchor and bounded zoom scale. Personal star optics/surfaces render in front. Turbulent disk emission and bent arcs are an artistic projection, not a relativistic ray tracer.
- Stellar near views use hot multi-scale granulation and a continuous perspective corona. Interior gas uses compact world-space lobes, an open cavity and 48 automatic/64 high-quality settled interior ray steps; telescope guidance remains native1254.
- Scene audio is original procedural synthesis, enabled only by a gesture. Slow stereo voicings, HRTF creation/edit/delete/event cues, filtered noise, depth-dependent reverberation and bounded gains follow depth/focus; mute, hidden tabs and event cancellation stop transient cues. Operation cues follow durable storage and current-identity checks.
